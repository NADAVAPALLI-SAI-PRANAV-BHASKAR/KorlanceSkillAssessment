Frequently Asked Questions

Candidate Help & Guidance

What happens if my internet connection drops during the examination?

The KORLANCE examination engine continuously saves every selected response in real time via secure background requests. If your network temporarily disconnects, you can reload the exam room URL without losing previously saved responses. Note that the 40-minute server timer continues to count down.

Can I navigate back and change answers before submitting?

Yes. Candidates have full freedom to move between questions using the question palette, Previous/Next buttons, clear answers, or mark questions for review. Responses are only locked upon clicking "Submit Examination" or when the 40-minute countdown reaches zero.

When will I receive my score and confirmation email?

Evaluation is instantaneous. Upon clicking submit, you are immediately redirected to your comprehensive performance results page displaying marks, accuracy breakdown, and qualifying category. An official confirmation email is automatically sent to your registered email address.
