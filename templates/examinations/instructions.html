1. General Examination Instructions

The examination duration is strictly 40 minutes, consisting of 50 multiple-choice questions (MCQs) with 2 marks per question and a maximum aggregate score of 100 marks. There is no negative marking for incorrect responses.

The examination questions are strictly tailored to your selected domain: {{ domain.name }}. A synchronized live countdown timer will be displayed prominently on your examination header. The assessment will be automatically submitted when the 40-minute duration expires.

2. Examination Navigation Instructions

Candidates can navigate between questions at any time using the right-hand question palette, radio buttons, or the action buttons:

Save & Next: Confirms your answer and proceeds to the next question.
Mark for Review & Next: Flags the question to revisit before final submission.
Clear Response: Erases your selected answer for the active question.
Question Palette: Click any numbered button (1–50) to jump directly.

3. Proctored Examination Rules & Academic Integrity

This is a monitored online examination. Candidates must keep their webcam active throughout the assessment duration.

  • Switching browser tabs or minimizing the examination window is strictly detected and recorded.
  • Opening external applications, developer tools (F12), or AI search engines is forbidden.
  • Multiple faces or prolonged absence from the camera frame will be flagged for controller audit.
  • Do not close or refresh the window; your server timer will not stop.
